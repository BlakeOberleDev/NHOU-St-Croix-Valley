02What is creep, and why are NHOU products effective over existing rust?

Creep: In undercoating, creep is a product's ability to move across a metal surface after application. Think of a drop of oil slowly spreading across paper. On a vehicle, that movement helps the product travel beyond the exact point where it was sprayed and reach seams, folds, and other hard-to-access areas.

Wicking ability: Because NH Oil stays soft and active, it can migrate into existing surface rust, displace moisture, and block oxygen from reaching the underlying metal. In general, the rustier a vehicle is, the more important this movement becomes. Softer products provide more creep and self-healing ability; harder, more durable coatings move less after application and depend more heavily on complete initial coverage. We assess each vehicle so we can match the product to the amount and type of rust present.

03What products do you offer?

Products. We offer NH Oil, V3, and BOSS WAX Hybrid, all engineered by New Hampshire Oil Undercoating (NHOU).

BOSS WAX Hybrid.

BOSS WAX Hybrid is a semi-permanent, oil-based coating designed for vehicles with little to no rust - typically newer vehicles, often up to about two years old in our area. Vehicles from salt-free climates may qualify at older ages. It sprays on as a liquid so it can penetrate to the metal, then cures within about 48 hours to a pliable, matte-black, drip-free finish. We apply BOSS WAX Hybrid to the exposed undercarriage, then fog the inside of the frame, body cavities, and other hard-to-reach areas with Clear NH Oil. The Clear NH Oil contains real peppermint essential oil, which is very effective at deterring mice.

NH Oil.

NH Oil is our most versatile option and can be used on everything from a clean frame to a vehicle with significant surface rust. It stays active, creeps into seams and crevices, displaces moisture, and self-heals as it moves. It is available in clear and black; the black formula helps improve the appearance of a rusty undercarriage. Both formulas contain real peppermint essential oil, making NH Oil very effective at deterring mice. Because NH Oil remains soft and active to allow for creeping/wicking ability, it is more susceptible to abrasion than a cured coating, so we recommend annual reapplication. We apply it to the underbody and fog it into cavities for comprehensive coverage.

V3.

V3 is a hybrid formula that combines oil, wax, and lanolin. It is engineered to provide the deep penetration of oil, the added durability of wax, and the natural moisture resistance of lanolin. Compared with NH Oil, V3 has a lower-drip finish and greater durability while still retaining the ability to penetrate and protect seams and folds. We continue to fog NH Oil into enclosed cavities because its fluidity allows it to make a true mist lightly covering all surfaces and creeping into the surrounding seams and folds. V3 also contains real peppermint essential oil, making it a strong option for protection from mice. We recommend annual reapplication.

04Which product is best for a rusty vehicle versus a vehicle with little to no rust?

The basic idea. The more existing rust a vehicle has, the more important creep and wicking ability become. A soft, active coating can migrate into seams, folds, and surface rust after application. A firmer coating, like Boss Wax Hybrid, offers greater durability, but it does not move as freely once cured. That's why the condition of the vehicle matters when choosing a product.

NH Oil. NH Oil can be used on virtually any vehicle, from a brand-new frame to one with significant surface rust. Its high creep and self-healing characteristics make it especially useful on rustier vehicles.

V3. V3 is a good fit for vehicles ranging from no rust up to moderate surface rust when you want more durability and a lower-drip finish than NH Oil.

BOSS WAX Hybrid. BOSS WAX Hybrid is intended for vehicles with little to no rust. In our area, that usually means a newer vehicle - often about two years old or newer - although older vehicles from salt-free climates may also qualify. Eligibility is based on the vehicle's condition at the initial service. We reserve the right to inspect the vehicle before recommending Hybrid and confirming warranty eligibility.

05Can't I just wash my vehicle to keep it from rusting?

Washing helps, but it can't prevent rust by itself. If oxygen can access the metal, rust will begin. Salt, moisture, and debris collect inside seams, frame rails, rocker panels, and other cavities that ordinary washing of the external underbody generally doesn't reach. The dirt traps the oxygen, salt, and moisture against the metal, accelerating oxidation.

NHOU recommends avoiding routine high-pressure underbody washes after undercoating with NH Oil or V3 because they accelerate wear on these soft protective coatings. A thorough cleaning before service can be helpful, but long-term protection requires consistent protection of the hidden areas where corrosion begins.

06Should I have you wash my vehicle before its first undercoating? What is the difference between the wash packages?

Is a wash recommended?

Ideally, the vehicle should be as clean as is practical before its first undercoating. This allows the product to reach the metal and hidden cavities most effectively. Our technicians know where dirt, salt, and debris tend to collect and have the equipment to flush those areas thoroughly. A wash isn't always necessary, but in many cases it's the best preparation for the first application.

The goal of the Full Chassis Flush is to get the dirt/debris out of the cavities so the oil we fog in can adhere to the metal immediately rather than having to wick through any buildup first. Assuming the buildup doesn't completely wall off various sections, blocking the oil from reaching all parts, the following are some reasons that we feel a Full Chassis Flush is of value before the first undercoating: 1) The oil can adhere directly to the frame and begin displacing oxygen and moisture without an unknown lag time as it wicks through dirt to get to the metal. 2) The dirt could shift, leaving dry spots that the oil then needs to creep into. This could create areas where the coverage is thinner. 3) The dust could conceivably tack up some of the product so not all of it makes it through to the frame.

External Underbody Wash.

Pressure wash → apply NHOU Salt-Brine Eliminator → allow it to dwell → pressure wash → rinse.

This service is useful for removing excessive dirt, salt, and road buildup from the exterior of the undercarriage. It's a good choice before a first undercoating when the exterior is dirty but the enclosed cavities are still relatively clean, or between applications after unusually dirty driving conditions.

Full Chassis Flush.

This includes the External Underbody Wash plus a thorough flush of accessible cavities such as doors, rocker panels, frame rails, hood, and tailgate. We generally begin recommending a Full Chassis Flush before the first undercoating when a vehicle is 10 years old or older, or when it has seen a lot of dirt-road or muddy use. NHOU Salt-Brine Eliminator.

This cleaner is formulated to dissolve and release road salt, brine, and the sticky additives used in modern de-icing solutions.

07How do I know if my vehicle needs needle scaling?

We recommend needle scaling when a vehicle has loose or flaking rust or a previous coating that is no longer firmly attached. NH Oil and V3 are active coatings that can creep around and beneath loose material, which can eventually cause that material to detach. Removing unstable rust or coating first helps the fresh undercoating reach solid material and leaves a thicker, more consistent protective layer in place.

10How long does the undercoating last?

NH Oil. Wear varies with mileage, driving conditions, weather, and exposure to abrasion, but NHOU recommends annual application to maintain adequate protection. Because NH Oil remains active and continues to migrate, the product will move into seams and porous surface rust over time in addition to self-healing to the best of its ability.

V3. V3 is still an active, soft coating, but its mineral oil, wax, and lanolin formulation gives it more durability and a lower-drip finish than NH Oil. It's a good option for vehicles that see more-than-average mileage or harsher use. We recommend annual inspection and reapplication.

BOSS WAX Hybrid. BOSS WAX Hybrid is a semi-permanent coating designed for long-term durability. To maintain the lifetime, transferable warranty, return every two years for cavity-oil service, inspection, and any qualifying minor touch-ups. Major repairs or accident damage may fall outside normal warranty touch-up coverage.

11When is the HD/Wheel-Well Guard package appropriate for my vehicle?

Wheel wells. We generally recommend NHOU Wheel-Well Guard (our HD treatment) when a vehicle does not have wheel-well liners. Wheel wells take a beating from gravel, salt, sand, and road spray, so they benefit from a thicker, more abrasion-resistant coating to even out wear time between the undercarriage and wheel wells.

HD Package. This is a good fit for a vehicle with up to moderate rust that is driven more than average or needs added protection in high-abrasion areas. The package extends HD protection beyond the wheel wells. When paired with V3, it provides a more durable overall system for demanding use; we'll recommend a service interval based on the vehicle and how it is driven.

13What do you mean by “tack up” in reference to NH Oil and V3?

Tacking up is the terminology we use to describe the process by which NH Oil and V3 collect a light layer of dust from drive time on dryer roads. They need this dust to complete themselves and reach their highest efficacy.

Esthetically speaking, the dust takes the products from shiny black to matte black. Practically speaking, dust benefits the products in the following three ways:

It takes the product from minimal drip to very little, usually no, drip.

It makes them less messy to work on/around.

These products are sacrificial barriers. They will take the beating from whatever is thrown up from the road - rocks, sand, dirt - rather than your vehicle taking it. However, with that beating, the product will slowly wear overtime and need to be reapplied on a yearly basis.
