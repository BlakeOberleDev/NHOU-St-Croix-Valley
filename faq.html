02What is creep, and why are NHOU products effective over existing rust?

Creep is a product's ability to move across a metal surface after application. Like a drop of oil spreading across paper, it helps the product travel beyond where it was sprayed and reach seams, folds, and other hard-to-access areas.

Wicking ability describes how NH Oil, while soft and active, can migrate into surface rust, displace moisture, and block oxygen from reaching the metal. This movement is especially important on rustier vehicles. Softer products offer more creep and self-healing; harder, more durable coatings move less once applied and depend more on complete initial coverage. We assess each vehicle to match the product to its condition.

03What products do you offer?

We offer NH Oil, V3, and BOSS WAX Hybrid, all engineered by New Hampshire Oil Undercoating (NHOU).

BOSS WAX Hybrid

A semi-permanent, oil-based coating designed for vehicles with little to light rust—typically newer vehicles, often up to about two years old in our area. Vehicles from salt-free climates may qualify at older ages. It sprays on as a liquid to penetrate to the metal, then cures in about 48 hours to a pliable, matte-black, drip-free finish. We apply it to the exposed undercarriage and fog Clear NH Oil into the frame, body cavities, and other hard-to-reach areas. Clear NH Oil contains real peppermint essential oil, making this system well suited for vehicles going into winter storage.

NH Oil

Our most versatile option, suitable for a clean frame or a vehicle with significant surface rust. It stays active, creeps into seams and crevices, displaces moisture, and self-heals as it moves. Clear and black formulas are available; black can also improve the appearance of a rusty undercarriage. Both contain real peppermint essential oil. Since NH Oil remains soft and active, it is more susceptible to abrasion than a cured coating, so we recommend annual reapplication. We apply it to the underbody and fog it into cavities.

V3

A hybrid of oil, wax, and lanolin, engineered for oil's deep penetration, wax's added durability, and lanolin's moisture resistance. Compared with NH Oil, V3 has a lower-drip finish and greater durability while still penetrating seams and folds. We fog NH Oil into enclosed cavities because its fluidity works especially well in hard-to-reach spaces. V3 also contains real peppermint essential oil, and we recommend annual reapplication.

04Which product is best for a rusty vehicle versus a vehicle with little to no rust?

The more rust a vehicle has, the more important creep and wicking become. A soft, active coating can migrate into seams, folds, and surface rust. A harder coating is more durable but moves less freely after curing, so the vehicle's condition matters.

NH Oil works on virtually any vehicle, from a brand-new frame to significant surface rust. Its high creep and self-healing are especially useful on rustier vehicles.

V3 suits vehicles with little to moderate surface rust when you want more durability and a lower-drip finish than NH Oil.

BOSS WAX Hybrid is intended for vehicles with little to light rust—usually newer vehicles in our area, often about two years old or newer. Older vehicles from salt-free climates may qualify. We inspect the vehicle before recommending Hybrid and confirming warranty eligibility.

05Can't I just wash my vehicle to keep it from rusting?

Washing helps, but it can't prevent rust by itself. Salt, moisture, and debris collect inside seams, frame rails, rocker panels, and cavities ordinary washing often cannot reach. NHOU recommends avoiding routine high-pressure underbody washes after oil undercoating because they can accelerate coating wear. A thorough cleaning before service can help, but long-term protection means treating the hidden areas where corrosion begins.

06Should I have you wash my vehicle before its first undercoating? What is the difference between the wash packages?

Is a wash recommended?

Ideally, the vehicle should be as clean as practical before its first undercoating so the product can reach metal and hidden cavities. Our technicians know where dirt, salt, and debris collect and can flush those areas thoroughly. A wash isn't always necessary, but often makes the best preparation for a first application.

External Underbody Wash

Pressure wash, apply NHOU Salt-Brine Eliminator and let it dwell, then pressure wash and rinse. This removes excessive dirt, salt, and road buildup from the exterior undercarriage. It's useful before a first undercoating when the exterior is dirty but cavities are relatively clean, or between applications after unusually dirty driving.

Full Chassis Flush

Includes the External Underbody Wash plus a thorough flush of accessible cavities such as doors, rocker panels, frame rails, hood, and tailgate. We generally begin recommending it before first undercoating when a vehicle is 10 years old or older, or has had substantial dirt-road or muddy use that may leave internal buildup.

NHOU Salt-Brine Eliminator

This cleaner is formulated to dissolve and release road salt, brine, and the sticky additives used in modern de-icing solutions.

07How do I know if my vehicle needs needle scaling?

We recommend needle scaling when a vehicle has loose or flaking rust, or a previous coating that is no longer firmly attached. NH Oil and V3 can creep around and beneath loose material, which may eventually cause it to detach. Removing unstable rust or coating first helps the fresh undercoating reach solid material and leaves a thicker, more consistent protective layer.

10How long does the undercoating last?

NH Oil: Wear varies with mileage, driving conditions, weather, and abrasion. NHOU recommends annual application to maintain adequate protection. Because NH Oil remains active and migrates, it can continue moving into seams and porous surface rust.

V3: This is still an active, soft coating, but its wax-and-lanolin hybrid formulation gives it more durability and a lower-drip finish than NH Oil. It's a good option for above-average mileage or harsher use. We recommend annual inspection and reapplication.

BOSS WAX Hybrid: A semi-permanent coating designed for long-term durability. To maintain the lifetime transferable warranty, return every two years for cavity-oil service, inspection, and qualifying minor touch-ups. Major repairs or accident damage may fall outside normal warranty touch-up coverage.

11When is the HD/Wheel-Well Guard package appropriate for my vehicle?

Wheel wells: We generally recommend NHOU Wheel-Well Guard (our HD treatment) when a vehicle lacks wheel-well liners or those areas see unusually high abrasion. Wheel wells, rocker areas, and other exposed surfaces take a beating from gravel, salt, sand, and road spray, so benefit from a thicker, more abrasion-resistant coating.

HD Package: A good fit for a vehicle with up to moderate rust that is driven more than average or needs added protection in high-abrasion areas. The package extends HD protection beyond the wheel wells. Paired with V3, it can provide a more durable overall system for demanding use. We'll recommend a service interval based on the vehicle and how it is driven.

12What are your aftercare instructions?

Aftercare for V3 and NH Oil

  • Application residue may reach the exhaust and smoke briefly the first time you drive; this is normal and will burn off. Residue on painted surfaces can be washed away.
  • Avoid routine high-pressure underbody washing, which creates unnecessary coating wear. Cleaning the undercarriage shortly before your next scheduled service is fine.
  • Some minimal dripping can occur shortly after application, especially with NH Oil. Park accordingly. Driving on dry roads can help a light layer of dust form and reduce dripping.
  • If NH Oil reaches door, hood, or other weatherstripping, wipe it away. Prolonged contact can swell some soft rubber. Surfactant-based automotive soap and a microfiber cloth will remove the product.
  • We recommend annual reapplication.

Aftercare for BOSS WAX Hybrid

  • A small amount of Clear NH Oil from enclosed cavities may drip shortly after application. Park accordingly. The Hybrid coating itself should be essentially drip-free after the initial 48-hour cure.
  • If Clear NH Oil reaches weatherstripping, wipe it away. Prolonged contact can swell some soft rubber. Surfactant-based automotive soap and a microfiber cloth or chenille wash mitt will remove it.
  • Avoid high-pressure underbody washes and off-roading for three weeks so the coating can fully settle. Rain is fine after the first 48 hours. If the coating is damaged, contact us for an inspection and appropriate touch-up.
  • To maintain the lifetime transferable warranty, return every two years (24 months) for a Clear NH Oil cavity refresh, coating inspection, and qualifying minor touch-ups. Routine minor Hybrid touch-ups are complimentary under the warranty; major damage or repairs may fall outside normal coverage.
