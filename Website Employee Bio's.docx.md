**Taden- Technician**

Born and raised in Luck, WI, Taden’s passion for vehicles started with his own mobile detailing business.  Through this endeavor he further observed the destructive capacity of rust.  Desiring to be part of the solution he joined our team in April 2026\.

That detailing background turned out to be the perfect foundation for undercoating, and he’s fully in his element here. When he’s not applying that attention to detail here at NHOU St Croix Valley, you’ll find him spending time with his girlfriend, enjoying music, sports or the great outdoors.   

**Marc- Owner & Technician**

Marc is originally from Vermont, where salt, brine and vehicle decay are just as common as they are here in beautiful Wisconsin.  A former Air Force Captain and mechanical engineer, he moved his family to the Midwest after joining Polaris ORV Engineering in 2018\.

Locals would ask about how he kept rust off his than 2003 Chevy.   Lots of conversations ensued.   It appeared there was a need for a service that was not prevalent in the area.  Tired of hearing “there is nothing you can do” and already familiar with the efficacy of NHOU products, Marc started coating vehicles at night on ramps in his garage.  After a few years there was no doubt this idea had potential. By 2023 NHOU St Croix Valley became the 3rd official NHOU franchise.  Initially only a mobile service the business grew into a physical location in Dec 2024 and ultimately to its current location in March of 2026\. 

Marc genuinely enjoys meeting customers, preserving vehicles, and keeping the Midwest’s trucks. In his spare time, he hangs out with his wife and four kids, tinkers with snowmobiles and cars, and oddly enjoys distance running.
