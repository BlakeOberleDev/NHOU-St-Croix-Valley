2. **What is creep, and why are NHOU products effective over existing rust?**

   1. **Creep.** In undercoating, creep is a product's ability to move across a metal surface after application. Think of a drop of oil slowly spreading across paper. On a vehicle, that movement helps the product travel beyond the exact point where it was sprayed and reach seams, folds, and other hard-to-access areas.

   2. **Wicking ability.** Because NH Oil stays soft and active, it can migrate into existing surface rust, displace moisture, and block oxygen from reaching the underlying metal. In general, the rustier a vehicle is, the more important this movement becomes. Softer products provide more creep and self-healing ability; harder, more durable coatings move less after application and depend more heavily on complete initial coverage. We assess each vehicle so we can match the product to the amount and type of rust present.

Vehicles from salt-free climates may qualify at older ages. It sprays on as a liquid so it can penetrate to the metal, then cures within about 48 hours to a pliable, matte-black, drip-free finish. We apply BOSS WAX Hybrid to the exposed undercarriage, then fog the inside of the frame, body cavities, and other hard-to-reach areas with Clear NH Oil. The Clear NH Oil contains real peppermint essential oil, making this system especially well suited for vehicles going into winter storage.

      2. **NH Oil.** NH Oil is our most versatile option and can be used on everything from a clean frame to a vehicle with significant surface rust. It stays active, creeps into seams and crevices, displaces moisture, and self-heals as it moves. It is available in clear and black; the black formula also helps improve the appearance of a rusty undercarriage. Both formulas contain real peppermint essential oil, making NH Oil an especially good choice for vehicles going into winter storage. Because NH Oil remains soft and active, it is more susceptible to abrasion than a cured coating, so we recommend annual reapplication. We apply it to the underbody and fog it into cavities for comprehensive coverage.

      3. **V3.** V3 is a hybrid formula that combines oil, wax, and lanolin. It is engineered to provide the deep penetration of oil, the added durability of wax, and the natural moisture resistance of lanolin. Compared with NH Oil, V3 has a lower-drip finish and greater durability while still retaining the ability to penetrate and protect seams and folds. We continue to fog NH Oil into enclosed cavities because its fluidity makes it especially effective in those hard-to-reach spaces. V3 also contains real peppermint essential oil, making it a strong option for winter storage. We recommend annual reapplication.

4. **Which product is best for a rusty vehicle versus a vehicle with little to no rust?**

   1. **The basic idea.** The more existing rust a vehicle has, the more important creep and wicking become. A soft, active coating can migrate into seams, folds, and surface rust after application. A harder coating offers greater durability, but it does not move as freely once cured. That's why the condition of the vehicle matters when choosing a product.

   2. **NH Oil.** NH Oil can be used on virtually any vehicle, from a brand-new frame to one with significant surface rust. Its high creep and self-healing characteristics make it especially useful on rustier vehicles.

   3. **V3.** V3 is a good fit for vehicles ranging from little rust up to moderate surface rust when you want more durability and a lower-drip finish than NH Oil.

   4. **BOSS WAX Hybrid.** BOSS WAX Hybrid is intended for vehicles with little to light rust. In our area, that usually means a newer vehicle \- often about two years old or newer \- although older vehicles from salt-free climates may also qualify. Eligibility is based on the vehicle's condition at the initial service, and we'll inspect the vehicle before recommending Hybrid and confirming warranty eligibility.

5. **Can't I just wash my vehicle to keep it from rusting?**

   1. Washing helps, but it can't prevent rust by itself. Salt, moisture, and debris collect inside seams, frame rails, rocker panels, and other cavities that ordinary washing often cannot reach. NHOU also recommends avoiding routine high-pressure underbody washes after oil undercoating because they can accelerate wear on the protective coating. A thorough cleaning before service can be helpful, but long-term protection requires treating the hidden areas where corrosion begins.

6. **Should I have you wash my vehicle before its first undercoating? What is the difference between the wash packages?**

   1. **Is a wash recommended?** Ideally, the vehicle should be as clean as practical before its first undercoating so the product can reach the metal and hidden cavities effectively. Our technicians know where dirt, salt, and debris tend to collect and have the equipment to flush those areas thoroughly. A wash isn't always necessary, but in many cases it's the best preparation for the first application.

   2. **External Underbody Wash.** Pressure wash \-\> apply NHOU Salt-Brine Eliminator \-\> allow it to dwell \-\> pressure wash \-\> rinse.

      1. This service is useful for removing excessive dirt, salt, and road buildup from the exterior of the undercarriage. It's a good choice before a first undercoating when the exterior is dirty but the enclosed cavities are still relatively clean, or between applications after unusually dirty driving conditions.

   3. **Full Chassis Flush.** This includes the External Underbody Wash plus a thorough flush of accessible cavities such as the doors, rocker panels, frame rails, hood, and tailgate.

      1. We generally begin recommending a Full Chassis Flush before the first undercoating when a vehicle is 10 years old or older, or when it has seen a lot of dirt-road or muddy use. Those conditions can create thick internal buildup that makes it harder for NH Oil to reach and adhere to the metal.

   4. **NHOU Salt-Brine Eliminator.** This cleaner is formulated to dissolve and release road salt, brine, and the sticky additives used in modern de-icing solutions.

7. **How do I know if my vehicle needs needle scaling?**

   1. We recommend needle scaling when a vehicle has loose or flaking rust or a previous coating that is no longer firmly attached. NH Oil and V3 are active coatings that can creep around and beneath loose material, which can eventually cause that material to detach. Removing unstable rust or coating first helps the fresh undercoating reach solid material and leaves a thicker, more consistent protective layer in place.

    1. **NH Oil.** Wear varies with mileage, driving conditions, weather, and exposure to abrasion, but NHOU recommends annual application to maintain adequate protection. 11.  **When is the HD/Wheel-Well Guard package appropriate for my vehicle?**

    1. **Wheel wells.** We generally recommend NHOU Wheel-Well Guard (our HD treatment) when a vehicle does not have wheel-well liners or when those areas see unusually high abrasion. Wheel wells, rocker areas, and other exposed surfaces take a beating from gravel, salt, sand, and road spray, so they benefit from a thicker, more abrasion-resistant coating.

    2. **HD Package.** This is a good fit for a vehicle with up to moderate rust that is driven more than average or needs added protection in high-abrasion areas. 12.  **What are your aftercare instructions?**

    1. **Aftercare for V3 and NH Oil:**

       1. Sometimes a small amount of residue from the application can reach the exhaust. It may smoke briefly the first time you drive; this is normal and will burn off. Any residue on painted surfaces can be washed away.

       2. Avoid routine high-pressure underbody washing. It creates unnecessary wear on the coating. Cleaning the undercarriage shortly before your next scheduled undercoating service is fine.

       3. Some minimal dripping can occur just after application, particularly with NH Oil. Park accordingly. Driving on dry roads can help the coating pick up a light layer of dust, which often reduces dripping more quickly.

       4. If NH Oil creeps out of drain holes and reaches the weatherstripping around doors, the hood, or other openings, wipe it away. Prolonged contact can cause some soft rubber weatherstripping to swell. A surfactant-based automotive soap and a microfiber cloth will remove the product.

       5. We recommend annual reapplication.

    2. **Aftercare for BOSS WAX Hybrid:**

       1. A small amount of Clear NH Oil from the enclosed cavities may drip shortly after application. Park accordingly. The BOSS WAX Hybrid coating itself should be essentially drip-free after the initial 48-hour cure period.

       2. If Clear NH Oil creeps out of drain holes and reaches weatherstripping around doors, the hood, or other openings, wipe it away. Prolonged contact can cause some soft rubber weatherstripping to swell. A surfactant-based automotive soap and a microfiber cloth or chenille wash mitt will remove the product.

       3. Avoid high-pressure underbody washes and off-roading for three weeks after application so the coating can fully settle. Driving in the rain is fine after the first 48 hours. If you notice damage to the coating, contact us and we'll be happy to inspect it and touch it up as appropriate.

       4. To maintain your lifetime transferable warranty, return every two years (24 months) for a refresh of the Clear NH Oil in the cavities, an inspection of the BOSS WAX Hybrid coating, and qualifying minor touch-ups. Under the warranty, routine minor BOSS WAX Hybrid touch-ups are complimentary; major damage or repairs may fall outside normal warranty coverage.
