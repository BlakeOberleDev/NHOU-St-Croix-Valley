Owners · Customer care

Marc & Kristiana

Marc Owner & Technician

Originally from Vermont, Marc is a former Air Force captain and mechanical engineer. After moving to the Midwest to work in Polaris ORV Engineering, he began coating vehicles in his garage, inspired by the rust protection he used on his own 2003 Chevy. NHOU St. Croix Valley grew from those early conversations into the local shop it is today.

Marc enjoys meeting customers and helping preserve their vehicles. Away from the shop, he spends time with his wife and four kids, tinkers with snowmobiles and cars, and enjoys distance running.

Kristiana Secretary

Also from Vermont, Kristiana brings a background in dental hygiene and physical therapy to the business. She helps customers, keeps the office running smoothly, and makes sure the details stay organized behind the scenes.

When she’s not at the shop, Kristiana homeschools their four kids, helps run the business, bakes cookies, hikes, and enjoys the outdoors.

Technician

Taden

Born and raised in Luck, Wisconsin, Taden’s interest in vehicles began with his own mobile detailing business. There, he saw firsthand the damage rust can cause and wanted to be part of the solution. He joined the team in April 2026, bringing a detail-oriented approach that makes undercoating a natural fit.

Outside the shop, Taden enjoys spending time with his girlfriend, listening to music, following sports, and getting outdoors.

Secretary

Jess

Jess was born in St. Croix Falls, grew up on a farm in Shafer, Minnesota, and now lives in the Sterling Barrens. With a background in psychology, biology, and neuroscience, she brings both scientific curiosity and a people-first approach to the shop.

A volunteer firefighter and wildlife-area volunteer, Jess loves learning how things work and helping others learn, too. In her free time, she enjoys hunting, fishing, gardening, and traveling.
