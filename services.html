Three formulas. One goal.

Protection that fits your vehicle.

Every vehicle and every winter is different. Compare how each formula creeps, cures, and holds up, then browse the full service menu below.

01

Maximum creep · Annual protection

NH Oil

A proven, oil-based shield that works its way into seams, cavities, and existing rust.

Choose clear oil or black oil for your undercarriage. With the lowest viscosity and up to 7 inches of creep, NH Oil is the best fit for severe rust and hard-to-reach internal cavities.

  • Best for Any rust level, especially heavier rust
  • Creep / viscosity Up to 7 inches; low viscosity
  • Feel Remains oily; strong self-healing
  • Cost / care Lowest upfront; annual application recommended

Some minimal dripping can occur just after application. The product needs drive time on dryer roads to tack up the exterior layer. Oil dye does not creep or self-heal.

02

Balanced formula · Built for hard use

V3

A thicker blend for drivers who want less drip and added durability without giving up the benefits of an oil-based coating.

NH Oil, wax, and lanolin combine for a mid-range viscosity suited to light or moderate rust. It creeps 3–4 inches and is a good choice for vehicles that see above-average mileage or harsher conditions.

  • Best for No rust to moderate rust and tougher use
  • Creep / viscosity 3–4 inches; mid-range viscosity
  • Feel Remains oily; good self-healing
  • Cost / care Mid-range; annual application recommended

Designed to drip much less than NH Oil, though minor dripping may still occur. Oil dye does not creep or self-heal.

03

Cured finish · Long-term durability

Boss Wax Hybrid

A premium semi-permanent coating designed for vehicles with little to no existing rust.

This higher-viscosity formula cures to a pliable finish within 48 hours and offers the longest-lasting protection of the three. Clear NH Oil is also applied to cavities, where it can continue to protect hard-to-reach areas.

  • Best for Vehicles with little to no rust
  • Creep / viscosity About 1 inch laterally; high viscosity
  • Feel Cures to a cold-butter consistency
  • Cost / care Highest upfront; Cheapest option over 5 years; Lifetime transferable warranty requires service every 2 years

The boss wax hybrid layer does not generally drip after 48 hours of cure time. However, the NH Oil fogged into the cavities will still need drive time on dryer roads to tack up. The coating has minimal self-healing.
